The real price

Waiting isn’t free. You’re already paying for it.

Yes, in the takeout you order because there’s no plan. But that’s the smallest cost.

The real cost is what food is quietly taking from you. It’s pulling you farther from God, because the thing you reach for first when you’re tired, anxious or lonely isn’t Him. It’s pulling you from the people you love, because you’re too drained, too foggy and too hard on yourself to be fully present with them.

And it’s stealing the energy, confidence and clarity you need for what God has already placed in your lap. The calling, the marriage, the children, the purpose. You can’t steward them well while you’re running on empty.

Your kids are paying too. The enemy has always used food as a door, from the very first bite in the garden. He uses it to whisper lies to our children about who they are, what their bodies are worth, and who God is. Every craving they’re ruled by and every bit of shame they feel at the table is a door left open.

‘Someday’ isn’t a plan. It’s how next January looks exactly like this one, with that door still open in your home.

It’s time to close it.

Why forty days

Forty days is how God prepares His people.

The rain fell for forty days. Moses was on the mountain forty days. Jesus spent forty days in the wilderness before His ministry began. Forty days is long enough to break an old pattern, and short enough that you can see the finish line from day one.

Daniel asked for just ten days to prove what God’s food would do. I’m giving your family forty.

Genesis 7:12 · Exodus 24:18 · Matthew 4:2 · Daniel 1:12

Questions

Before you join

When does it start?

January 1. Join now and your seat is saved, and you’ll get what you need to get ready before day one.

My husband and kids are picky. Will this work?

That’s exactly who I built it for. The swaps are made for the whole table, and the Little Learners lessons bring your kids into it instead of fighting them at dinner.

What if my husband isn’t on board yet?

You don’t need him to sign up. You need him to sit down and eat. Most husbands come around the first time a meal is good and nobody calls it diet food.

How much time will it take?

The thinking is done for you. You get the meal plan, the grocery list and one prep day a week. You cook, I handle the planning.

I’ve tried before and fallen off.

You didn’t fail. The system failed you. This one has a weekly plan, daily affirmations, a weekly devotional, weekly check-ins and a private group chat of families walking it with you. And if you slip, you don’t start over. You come back to the table.

Is now really the right time?

There’s never a perfect time to change how your family eats. January 1 is the closest thing to one. And the price only gets better if you decide early.

Is there a payment plan?

Yes, you can split it into 3 payments, charged every 2 weeks. You’ll see the option right under the price.

Can I give it as a gift?

Yes. Check out with your details, then email kaylabundymanagement@gmail.com with the name and email of the person you’re gifting it to, and I’ll set them up.

Is this medical advice?

No. This is faith and whole food, not medical advice. If you’re pregnant, nursing, managing a health condition or taking medication, talk to your doctor before making big changes.
